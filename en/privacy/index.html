TravElly

Privacy Policy

TravElly collects no personal data, needs no account, and stores nothing outside your device. What you make in the app stays yours.

About TravElly

TravElly is a travel app for families with kids ages 6 to 14. Parents set up the trip. Kids see where the family is going, add activities themselves, and keep a diary with photos and stories along the way.

Data and privacy

TravElly respects your privacy completely:

  • All data stays on your device
  • Nothing is shared with the developer or with third parties
  • No account is needed and nothing is stored in the cloud
  • Information only leaves the device when you use the share button yourself. You choose the app and the recipient
  • TravElly contains no ads and no tracking
  • No analytics or diagnostic data is collected
  • No data is used for profiling or advertising

Photo library access

TravElly asks for access to your photo library only to add photos to the travel diary. Access is requested the first time you select a photo. Photos are stored locally on the device and are never shared or forwarded automatically.

Who it's for

TravElly is designed for kids ages 6 to 14. Parents set up the trip and give their kids a picture of the vacation ahead. Kids then plan along and fill their own diary with photos and stories.

The app can live on a parent's phone, so kids use it together or with supervision. That fits families who prefer limited or controlled smartphone use. You can also install TravElly directly on your child's phone. You decide what works for your family.

TravElly collects no personal data from children and requires no registration or account.

Accessibility

TravElly follows your iPhone's dark mode setting automatically. The calm, dark color scheme reduces visual strain. People with light sensitivity or an acquired brain injury may especially benefit from it, as does anyone who prefers softer light.

Sharing

TravElly offers a share button that uses the standard iOS share sheet. You choose the app and the recipient yourself. Nothing is shared automatically, and TravElly has no access to what happens with the content after you share it.
